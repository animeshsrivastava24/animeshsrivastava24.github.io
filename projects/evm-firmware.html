Electronic voting machine firmware

STM32 firmware on FreeRTOS for an electronic voting machine.

2020FreeRTOSSTM32CUARTI2C
1Problem
A voting machine has to record every vote safely, notice when someone tampers with it, and keep the count protected until it is officially read.
2Solution
Firmware on an STM32 running FreeRTOS that keeps vote capture, secure storage and anti-tamper monitoring running side by side, with the count protected from being read or changed early. Peripherals talk over UART and I2C.
