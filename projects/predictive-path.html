Predictive Path Analyzer

An embedded system that clears the way for emergency vehicles. Won the KPIT Sparkle Most Popular Project award (INR 1 lakh prize).

2018EmbeddedTraffic optimization
1Problem
Ambulances and fire engines lose critical minutes waiting at red lights and in traffic on the way to an emergency.
2Solution
A green-route optimizer: it predicts the path an emergency vehicle will take and gives it green signals along that route, so the road ahead clears before the vehicle arrives.
