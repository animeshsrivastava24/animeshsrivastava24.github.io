Custom bootloader for STM32

A bare-metal bootloader on the STM32 Nucleo-F4 that updates firmware in the field over UART, and never jumps into a broken image.

2025Embedded CSTM32UARTCRC
1Problem
A board that is already deployed needs new firmware, but there is no debugger attached. A half-written or corrupted image must not leave the device unable to boot.
2Solution
A bare-metal bootloader that owns the first part of flash. On power-on it checks whether an update is pending, receives the new image over UART and writes flash only after the image passes a CRC check.
  • Verifies the stored application with a CRC before every jump
  • Stays in the bootloader and reports an error if either check fails
  • Hands over to the application by setting VTOR, MSP and PC
